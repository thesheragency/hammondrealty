Buyer Tips

Taking The Stress Out Of Buying

Setting a few clear ground rules early protects your cash and saves you months of wasted weekend tours.

Start with a Strategy Call

We map out a game plan tailored to your specific goals before you start tracking public sites.

The 5-Year Reality Check

Buy for your current lifestyle, but ensure the property can adapt so you aren't forced to move again too soon.

Ignore the Ugly Wallpaper

Separate major structural issues from minor surface updates. Outdated kitchens are easy to fix but a flawed layout is permanent.

Lock In Your Pre-Approval

Sellers will not take you seriously without a verified commitment showing you are qualified to close from day one.

Write a Tight, Clean Offer

Price matters, but fast timelines and protective, zero-fluff contract conditions are what actually win bidding wars.

Budget 3% for Closing Costs

Expect an extra 2% to 3% beyond your down payment to safely cover your title, escrow, and lender fees.
