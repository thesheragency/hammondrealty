# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages (`/`, `/buyer`, `/seller`, `/about`, `/connect`, `/faqs`, `/home-prep-program`, `/home-value-analysis`, `/book-consultation`)
- WordPress-driven public pages under `app/[slug]`
- Blog listing and blog post routes (`/blog`, `/blog/[slug]`)
- Public utility pages that can be reached directly and affect indexation (`/thank-you`, `/booked`, `/privacy-policy`)
- Crawlability assets (`/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/sitemap_index.xml`, `/wp-sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated/admin/preview/API routes
- Internal/demo/debug routes (`/style-guide`, `/landing-demo`, `/debug-hydration`)

## Target audience
- Home buyers, home sellers, and homeowners in the greater Sacramento region seeking real-estate guidance and services.
